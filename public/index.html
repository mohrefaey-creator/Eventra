Good to know: phones, iPads and Bluetooth
  • Wi-Fi, not Bluetooth. Bluetooth is far too slow to carry live screen video, and browsers can't use it for that. Both devices just need to be on the same Wi-Fi (or have internet).
  • Browser limits. iPhone and iPad Safari, and most Android browsers, can't capture their own screen from a web page. Desktop browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari on Mac) can.
  • To mirror a phone or tablet use the system feature (AirPlay on iPhone/iPad, Cast on Android) or a native sender app speaking the MirrorLink protocol (see docs/PROTOCOL.md).
